01 // CONTEXT

About The Observatory

The Tempus Omnia Revelat Observatory investigates emerging technological and societal change through structured observation, research, synthesis, and comparative perspective. Artificial intelligence is reshaping how people work, how organisations operate, and how decisions are made.

Progress demands more than speed — it requires disciplined observation, historical context, research, and multiple perspectives.

We help organisations understand:

➔ What is changing
➔ Why it matters
➔ What may be affected
➔ Emerging assumptions & dependencies
➔ Lessons from previous transitions
➔ How to respond responsibly
// OUR CORE QUESTION
"How do we remain capable of understanding what AI is changing while we are participating in the change itself?"
// OUR ROLE

We help clients examine evidence, clarify context, challenge assumptions, identify uncertainty, and weigh implications. The final decisions always remain yours.

04 // SPECIFICATIONS

Core Services Detailed

LEVEL 01 — EXPLORE From £750

Research Brief

A focused, independent investigation into a clearly defined question concerning AI, emerging technology, organisational change, or wider technological and societal transition. Provides structured perspective before a client decides what to do.

Typical Questions:

  • What is happening & what do we actually know?
  • What evidence exists and what has changed?
  • What competing interpretations exist?
  • Why might this matter & what should we investigate?

OUTPUT & DELIVERY:

• Concise Research Brief (5–15 pages)

• Executive summary, evidence, comparative perspective, findings, and questions for further consideration

Timescale: 7–10 working days

LEVEL 02 — UNDERSTAND From £2,500

AI Transition Assessment

Examines how the introduction, expansion, or increasing dependency upon AI affects an organisation, function, process, or operating environment. Shifts focus from "Can AI perform this task?" to "What happens when the organisation begins to rely upon AI performing this task?"

Areas Examined:

  • Capability & Organisational Readiness
  • Emerging Dependency & Operational Exposure
  • Human & Organisational Impact
  • Governance & Accountability Considerations

OUTPUT & DELIVERY:

• Comprehensive Assessment Report (15–30 pages)

• Structured picture of current situation, emerging transitions, findings, and key attention areas

Timescale: 2–4 weeks

LEVEL 03 — DECIDE From £5,000

Strategic Perspective & Advisory

Provides independent, structured perspective to leadership facing significant technological, strategic, or organizational decisions. Helps decision-makers examine what they know, what they don't know, assumptions, alternatives, and long-term consequences.

Typical Questions & Focus:

  • Should we adopt this technology now, later, or not at all?
  • What are we potentially committing ourselves to?
  • What would be difficult or impossible to reverse?
  • What assumptions must be true?

WORK DELIVERED:

• Decision definition & Evidence review

• Assumption testing & Alternative comparison

• Dependency & Irreversibility analysis

• Strategic advisory sessions & Consequence analysis

LEVEL 04 — ENGAGE From £1,500

Workshops & Executive Sessions

Brings the Observatory's structured perspective directly into your organisation for situations where leadership needs to examine difficult questions, challenge assumptions, and think together.

Workshop Themes:

  • Understanding the AI transition
  • AI capability vs organisational readiness
  • AI dependency & Responsible adoption
  • Decision-making under technological uncertainty

EXECUTIVE SESSION MODEL:

Input → Research → Structured Perspective → Discussion → Action

Prior research conducted beforehand to ensure high-value facilitation.
