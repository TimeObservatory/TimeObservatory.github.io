01 // Mandate

Independent Intelligence Practice

Blacklight Intelligence brings together intelligence collection, investigation, analysis and publication. We examine organisations, markets, technologies, events and claims to understand what the available information can establish — and where important questions remain open.

The work may begin with a public record, a commercial question, a developing technology, a set of conflicting accounts or a subject that has not yet received careful examination. Each inquiry is shaped by its question, the evidence available and the limits of what can responsibly be concluded.

  • Evidence and source context considered before conclusions are drawn
  • Corroboration, contradiction and alternative explanations examined
  • Clear distinction between fact, inference, hypothesis and uncertainty
  • Findings communicated in a form suited to the question and audience
02 // Approach

From Information to Understanding

Information is a starting point, not a conclusion. Blacklight examines material in context, tests the strength of the available support and considers what the evidence does — and does not — justify saying.

Define→ Collect→ Verify→ Analyse→ Report
  • Investigative rather than assumption-led
  • Open to competing explanations and correction
  • Focused on useful findings, not dramatic presentation
  • Scope and conclusions remain proportionate to the evidence

05 // Engagement

How an Inquiry Begins

A proportionate process helps establish whether the question can be investigated, what material may be available and what form of work is appropriate.

STEP 01

Define the question

Clarify the issue, the context and what the client needs to understand.

STEP 02

Assess the scope

Consider source availability, constraints, objectives and practical boundaries.

STEP 03

Agree the work

Confirm the proposed scope, deliverable, timescale and fee before work begins.

STEP 04

Report the findings

Present supported findings, relevant context, limitations and open questions.

No investigation or commission is implied by an initial enquiry. Any engagement would be subject to an agreed scope, appropriate terms and the applicable legal and ethical requirements.

06 // Standards

Integrity Without Theatre

A compelling story is not proof. A pattern does not, by itself, establish causation. Confidence should follow the quality of the evidence, not the force of the narrative.

Evidence before assertion Claims should be proportionate to the material supporting them.
Verification before confidence Sources and corroboration matter more than volume alone.
Context before interpretation Information is examined within its relevant setting.
Inference kept visible Observation, interpretation and hypothesis are not interchangeable.
Uncertainty acknowledged Limits and unresolved questions are stated rather than concealed.
Responsible investigation Work must respect applicable law, privacy and confidentiality obligations.
